# Third-party components

`index.html` embeds the following, unmodified, so the app works without internet. Each keeps its own licence.

| Component | Version | Used for | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SheetJS Community Edition (`xlsx`) | 0.18.5 | Reading and writing Excel workbooks; reading legacy Word (.doc) files | Apache License 2.0 |
| mammoth.js | 1.12.3 | Reading Word (.docx) blotters | BSD 2-Clause License |
| jsPDF | 2.5.1 | Digest, intel report and dossier PDFs | MIT License |
| Barlow and Barlow Condensed (Fontsource) | 5.3.0 | Typefaces | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

Full licence texts are published with each project:

- SheetJS: https://github.com/SheetJS/sheetjs/blob/master/LICENSE
- mammoth.js: https://github.com/mwilliamson/mammoth.js/blob/master/LICENSE
- jsPDF: https://github.com/parallax/jsPDF/blob/master/LICENSE
- Barlow: https://github.com/jpt/barlow/blob/main/OFL.txt
